About Me

Hello and welcome to my website! I am a 4th year theoretical physics student at Trinity College Dublin. I'm interested in using computational techniques to learn about how underlying mathematical structures can affect large systems. In particular, I've completed some research at Trinity College Dublin and later at DIAS on investigating the effects of perturbing a HXXZ Heisenberg spin chain, and how this changes with length. I have also looked at matrix models for membrane systems and how gauging the action of the bosonic BFSS model can affect its critical temperature and thermodynamic quantities.
